Bio

Sirarpi Mehrabyan

Hello and thank you for stopping by! My name is Sirarpi Mehrabyan. I’m a passionate crochet artist from Armenia. I’m the founder of the Etsy shop CROCHETandPATTERNS1, where I share my passion for handcrafted crochet pieces and original, easy-to-follow patterns. Crochet has been a part of my life for over 25 years—it’s more than a craft to me; it’s a way of sharing warmth, beauty, and tradition. I specialize in designing detailed crochet patterns for home decor, including elegant ladies, angels, nativity sets, and festive trees. My inspiration comes from classical art, vintage fashion, and the spirit of the holidays. Each pattern I create includes not just written instructions, but also step-by-step photos and diagrams to guide you clearly. My goal is to make your crochet experience both joyful and fulfilling—no matter your skill level. Thank you for choosing handmade and supporting independent creators like me!
